How Radhanite works

Circle tells the agent what it can buy and how to pay. Radhanite decides what is worth buying.

Economic execution layer

Autonomous work needs a control plane, not a bigger wallet.

Radhanite keeps value, budget, probability, and evidence in one provider-neutral decision loop. Integrations execute the selected capability; they do not rank themselves.

Radhanite does not predict outcomes. It controls the unit economics of autonomous work.
